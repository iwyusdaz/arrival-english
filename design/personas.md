# Personas

Fictional personas for design decisions. They are not real people or research participants.

| Persona | Need | What the design gives them |
|---|---|---|
| **Yusuf**, 34, newcomer, A2 | Survival tasks; anxious about speaking to strangers | No timers; odd and wrong answers still reach the goal; the NPC's reaction shows the effect of tone without failing the task; repair buttons on every turn; speech input optional |
| **Mei**, 19, international student, A2+ | Listening practice at natural speed, plus repair phrases | Every line can be heard (browser voice) at 0.75-1.25x; "Listen again"; listening check with transcripts revealed after answering; B1 "check understanding" options |
| **Ms Clarke**, ESL teacher | A pre-lesson task and a debrief guide | Teacher guide with timings, debrief questions and the language behind each task; learners can show the post-task table or download their record |
| **Deaf or hard-of-hearing learner** | A full reading-based path with equivalent tasks | All dialogue is text first (voice is extra); NPC reactions are written out in words; reading-equivalent listening items (signs, screens, emails) with the same communicative goals, labelled as an alternative, not equivalent listening practice |
| **Phone learner on a bus** (added during design) | Short sessions, one hand, no sound | Portrait layout with the dialogue panel below a scene band; progress saved locally; works muted |
